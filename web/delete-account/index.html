Delete Your Account

TaskCaster Party · Effective 7 September 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your TaskCaster Party account and the data attached to it, on Android, iOS and the web — whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open TaskCaster Party and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning and tap Delete My Account to confirm.
  5. If you signed in a while ago, we may ask you to confirm your identity again first (re-enter your password, or continue with Google/Apple) — this is a Firebase security requirement, not an extra step we added.

Deletion happens immediately: your account is signed out and cannot be signed back into once the process completes.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, open an issue at github.com/sagearbor/taskcaster-app/issues titled "Delete my account", including the email address or display name on the account so we can find it.

Requests submitted this way are processed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (email/password, Google or Apple sign-in link, guest ID)Permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication. You cannot sign back into it.
Profile (display name, avatar, account-creation date)Deleted.
Friends listDeleted. (A friend who added you keeps their own copy of that connection, pointing at an account that no longer exists.)
Push-notification device tokensDeleted — no further notifications are sent to any of your devices.
Games you created or playedNot deleted. They remain visible to the other players in those games so their history and scores stay intact, but your identity is disconnected from them going forward.
Community tasks you submittedRemain visible (they were shared content), no longer linked to your account.
This cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted there is no way for us to restore it or the data listed above.
Guest (anonymous) accounts that were never turned into a full account can also be deleted the same way — open the app while signed in as that guest and use the same Settings flow.
