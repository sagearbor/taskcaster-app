Privacy Policy

TaskCaster Party · Effective 7 September 2026

TaskCaster Party ("the app", "we") is an independent, fan-made party-game app for Android, iOS and the web. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the app at taskmaster-app-3d480.web.app and to the Android and iOS builds of the same app.

In short: we collect only what the game needs (an account, your display name, and what you and your friends do in your games). We show no ads, we sell no data, and we run no analytics or ad-tracking SDKs.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Guest play: you can play without creating an account. The app creates an anonymous account ID so your games can be saved. No name or email is required.
  • Registered accounts: if you sign up, we store the email address and display name you provide, or the name and email shared by Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, together with the sign-in provider's user ID.

Game content you create

  • Game names, invite codes, task lists, and the players in each game.
  • Your task submissions: links to videos you host elsewhere (for example Google Photos or YouTube), written answers, and drawings made in Drawing Telephone.
  • Scores, judging decisions, and community tasks you submit.
  • Friend lists and game invites you send or receive.

Other players in the same game can see the content you submit to that game. Games you mark as public are visible to other users of the app.

Device information

  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging is stored so we can notify you about your games.
  • Install referrer (Android): if you install the app from a friend's invite link, the invite code is read once from the Google Play install referrer so you land in the right game. Nothing else is read.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game: create and join games, sync progress between players, show scoreboards, and deliver invites.
  • To keep your games attached to your account across devices.
  • To send notifications you have opted into.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with data brokers.

3. Service providers

The app is built on Google Firebase. Your data is stored and processed by:

  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in and account management.
  • Cloud Firestore — game, profile, friend and community-task data.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications.
  • Firebase Hosting — the web version of the app.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, those providers process your sign-in under their own privacy policies. Video links you submit point to third-party services (for example Google Photos or YouTube) that you chose; those services' policies apply to the videos themselves.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • Your account and game data are kept until you delete them.
  • Guest (anonymous) accounts that are never upgraded may be removed after a period of inactivity.
  • You can delete your account and its data by contacting us (see below). We will remove your profile, friend links and notification tokens; your name is removed from games you took part in, though the games themselves remain for the other players.

5. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored in Firebase with access rules that restrict each user to their own profile and to games they are a member of. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights; we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced in the app.

9. Contact

Questions, data requests and deletion requests: github.com/sagearbor/taskcaster-app/issues

TaskCaster Party is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with any television programme or its producers.
